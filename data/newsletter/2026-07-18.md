The most compelling divergence this week is found in **Iowa soybeans**. The NDVI velocity of **+0.26147/16d** is the highest among all monitored nodes, yet the price is already at **99.0 %** of its 52‑week high. The velocity z‑score, calculated from the 2000‑2015 baseline, sits at **–6.9**, indicating a sharp decline in greenness relative to the long‑term mean, but the positive velocity shows a rapid rebound. In **Kansas wheat**, the velocity of **+0.22308/16d** and a z‑score of **–2.54** indicate a moderate improvement in greenness, but the price is at its 52‑week high. The divergence here is less pronounced but still signals that the market may be over‑valuing wheat relative to the satellite fundamentals.  

**São Paulo sugar** shows a velocity of **+0.21074/16d** with a z‑score of **8.43**, firmly in the positive quadrant. The price is only 87.5 % of its 52‑week high, implying that the market is under‑pricing sugar relative to the strong greening trend.

Overall, the **spectral alpha** is strongest in Iowa soybeans, where the positive velocity outpaces the price’s proximity to its 52‑week high. This misalignment presents a tactical opportunity for a short position if the price remains near the high while the velocity continues to climb.
