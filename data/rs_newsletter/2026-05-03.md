## 🌾 **Agriculture** & Crop Monitoring  
No new articles were published in the agriculture category this week.  
We continue to track crop phenology using NDVI and EVI indices derived from **Sentinel‑2** and Landsat 8, which measure vegetation greenness and vigor.  
Overlaying satellite imagery with **GPS‑derived field boundaries** highlights spatial variability in crop health across your acreage.  
**SMAP** provides 9‑kilometer resolution soil moisture data available through the [NASA Earthdata portal](https://earthdata.nasa.gov/), offering bulk download options.  
Combining soil moisture with temperature and precipitation data yields comprehensive risk assessment.  

## 🌊 **Flood** & Water Hazards  
The flood monitoring feed returned no new entries this week.  
SAR imagery from **Sentinel‑1** remains a powerful tool for detecting inundation during cloud‑filled periods.  
Generate flood masks by thresholding backscatter values and comparing them to historical baselines.  
Near‑real‑time alerts are published daily by the [Global Flood Monitoring System](https://globalfloodmonitoring.org/), which combines satellite observations with hydrological models.
