## 🛰 Remote Sensing  

SAR radar, which penetrates cloud cover that blocks optical satellites, has become the backbone of modern field monitoring.  
The Sentinel‑1 constellation now offers updated imagery every six days, cutting the previous 12‑day revisit time in half and enabling farmers to track crop stress, irrigation deficits, and early signs of disease with unprecedented speed.  
Because the data are freely available through NASA Earthdata, agronomists and insurers can integrate it into decision‑support tools without incurring costly licensing fees.  
**Practical implication:** Farm managers and risk assessors should start incorporating six‑day SAR updates into their crop‑health dashboards to catch problems before they translate into yield loss.
